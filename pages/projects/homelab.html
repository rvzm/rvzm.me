Homelab

Two machines with clearly split jobs: one thinks, one remembers.

kelvin

The compute box: 4 EPYC cores, 6 GB of RAM, managed through Webmin and Cockpit. It runs the websites, the code, and anything public-facing — including this page.

It happily runs zboe2's main live site, the wiki for zboe2, and will host future projects.

vulkan

The storage side. Bulk capacity over speed, mounted where kelvin can reach it.

This is responsible for offsite backups, media storage, and eventually a CDN for my projects.
