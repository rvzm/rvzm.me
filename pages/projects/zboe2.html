# About zboe2 ### Zombie Biohazard Outbreak Experiment 2 *One world. Countless possibilities. How will you survive?* ## What is zboe2? **zboe2** is an open-source, browser-based multiplayer survival role-playing game that combines zombie hunting, exploration, resource gathering, character progression, and community-driven gameplay. Originally conceived as an IRC-based zombie-hunting game, zboe2 takes the simplicity and accessibility of classic text-based multiplayer games and expands upon them with a modern graphical interface, interconnected world, and extensive RPG mechanics. There are no large downloads or complicated installations required to play. All you need is a web browser and a willingness to survive. ## The Experiment At the heart of zboe2 is a shared world threatened by an evolving zombie outbreak. What begins with isolated wandering zombies can escalate into dangerous hordes, full-scale raids, and sieges against the survivors' Basecamp. Players must manage their equipment, ammunition, health, shields, and resources while deciding when to fight, when to retreat, and when to work together. Survival is never guaranteed. Death carries real consequences, and even the collective safety of Basecamp can be lost. ## Play Your Own Way Surviving the outbreak doesn't necessarily mean spending every moment hunting zombies. Players can explore the world, develop specialized skills, manufacture equipment, learn magic, and contribute to the survival of others. Features include: - **Dynamic zombie combat** — Engage wandering zombies, break hordes, and overcome devastating raids. - **Character progression** — Earn experience, improve your attributes, and develop ten independently trainable skills. - **Exploration** — Travel between interconnected locations, each offering unique resources, activities, and dangers. - **Crafting and gathering** — Fish, mine, harvest resources, forge equipment, cook food, and brew potions. - **Weapons and equipment** — Maintain firearms, manage ammunition, upgrade equipment, and equip protective armor. - **Magic** — Discover and learn spells for combat, protection, healing, assistance, and transportation. - **Quests** — Complete objectives and challenges to earn rewards and advance your character. - **Multiplayer interaction** — Communicate through global chat, cooperate against shared threats, and compete on the experience leaderboard. Become a dedicated zombie hunter, a skilled craftsman, a resource gatherer, a powerful magic user, or a little of everything. Your approach to survival is your own. ## Built to Be Open Developed by **rvzm**, zboe2 is an independent project built using Node.js, Express, SQLite, and standard web technologies. Released under the GNU General Public License v3.0, its source code is publicly available, allowing anyone to explore its inner workings, contribute improvements, modify the game, or host their own instance. The project embraces the accessibility, simplicity, and community-oriented spirit of traditional browser and IRC gaming while introducing deeper gameplay systems. ## An Evolving World zboe2 is under active development. New mechanics, locations, items, quests, and gameplay opportunities may be introduced as the project evolves. As an early-development game, mechanics may change, balancing may be adjusted, and progress resets may occasionally be necessary. The Experiment is far from finished. **Will you fight the outbreak, build a life within it, or simply see how long you can survive?** *Welcome to zboe2.
