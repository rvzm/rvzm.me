# About Apocylta ### A post-apocalyptic RPG, for your terminal. *The world is unforgiving. Your future is unwritten.* ## What is Apocylta? **Apocylta** is an open-source, single-player, terminal-based role-playing game that blends post-apocalyptic survival with traditional fantasy, exploration, character progression, and resource management. Built around a fully interactive, hotkey-driven terminal interface, Apocylta embraces the simplicity of classic text-based gaming without sacrificing the depth and complexity of a modern RPG. There are no elaborate graphics, enormous downloads, or demanding hardware requirements. Just you, your terminal, and a world waiting to be explored. ## A World Worth Exploring The lands of Apocylta stretch across bustling cities, abandoned settlements, dangerous wilderness, underground mining networks, coastal communities, and ancient castles. With more than 70 interconnected locations, each offering different resources, opportunities, inhabitants, and dangers, exploration is an essential part of your adventure. Travel by foot, airboat, or magical teleportation. Discover new settlements, establish trade routes, investigate forgotten places, and face powerful enemies lurking in the world's deepest chambers. A functioning world clock, changing weather, and scheduled shop hours make the world feel alive beyond your immediate actions. ## Forge Your Own Path There is no single way to experience Apocylta. Your character develops through the activities you choose to pursue, allowing you to establish your own identity within the world. Features include: - **Character customization** — Choose from five races, five classes, seven starter packs, and customizable skill proficiencies. - **Skill progression** — Develop your character through a system of 17 skills, covering everything from combat and magic to gathering and trade. - **Exploration and gathering** — Scavenge, forage, mine, fish, trap wildlife, and harvest natural resources. - **Crafting and production** — Discover over 1,000 items, forge equipment, prepare meals, brew potions, and refine raw materials. - **Turn-based combat** — Encounter over 100 enemies, ranging from wandering creatures to formidable bosses. - **Magic** — Discover dozens of spells for combat, healing, assistance, and transportation. - **Trade and economy** — Buy and sell goods, discover rare equipment, and explore the underground black market. - **Player-owned housing** — Purchase a home, install crafting stations, and establish your own base of operations. - **Quests and achievements** — Complete objectives, overcome challenges, and earn rewards. Become a wealthy merchant, an accomplished blacksmith, a legendary warrior, a powerful mage, or a fisherman making a comfortable living along the coast. The choice is yours. ## Survival on Your Terms Apocylta features seven difficulty settings, ranging from Casual to Demon Lord. Difficulty influences more than the strength of your enemies. Resource availability, experience gains, skill proficiencies, encounter frequency, and the consequences of death all change depending on your chosen experience. Play at your own pace, or challenge yourself in a world where a single fatal mistake can permanently end your adventure. ## Beyond the Terminal While gameplay takes place entirely within your terminal, Apocylta also features a companion browser-based Playercard. View your character's statistics, equipment, inventory, quests, and achievements in real time, or export your character profile to share with others. Local save slots and automatic saving allow you to maintain multiple adventures without requiring an online account or external game server. ## Built to Be Open Developed by **rvzm**, Apocylta is an independent project built using Node.js, SQLite, and neo-blessed. Released under the GNU General Public License v3.0, the game is publicly available for players, developers, and enthusiasts to explore, modify, and contribute to. Apocylta remains under active development, with its world and gameplay systems continuing to evolve. The apocalypse may have shaped the world, but it doesn't have to define your place within it. **Survive. Explore. Prosper.
